Hazel St Forecast

The storm

A fast Alberta clipper crosses Thursday morning with a quick 3–6″ for the Greens and western Maine. Behind it, northwest flow sets up Friday and Saturday — classic upslope that piles snow on the northern spine (Sugarbush, Killington, Sugarloaf) well past what the models print. The southern mountains sit south of the best forcing and start Thursday mixed at the base.

Sunday dries out; a weaker system brings 2–4″ Monday. Models agree on Thursday, split on how long the upslope lasts — treat the 5-day totals as low confidence.
