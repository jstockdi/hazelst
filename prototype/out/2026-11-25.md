## The storm

High pressure keeps today and Thanksgiving morning dry and cold — lows in the
teens, the coldest of the season so far (NWS Gray). A coastal low forms off
Florida tonight, runs up the coast, and reaches the Gulf of Maine by early
Friday. It is a warm storm for skiing: warm air arrives aloft first, so cold air
trapped in the mountains turns the onset to **freezing rain Thursday night**
(8 PM – 4 AM per NWS Burlington). Friday, rain reaches the summits at
Sugarbush, Stratton, and Loon, while Killington, Pico, Sunday River, and
Sugarloaf stay icy up top.

Models agree it brings about an inch of liquid and almost no snow: the 3-day
summit snow is 0–0.3″ everywhere. A little snow comes back on the back side
Saturday (up to about 1″ at Killington and Stratton). NWS Burlington expects
seasonably mild weather next week, with showers Sunday night into Monday.

## Mountain by mountain

**Killington.** Dry and cold through Thursday midday; summit gusts reach about
44 mph Thursday afternoon, then freezing rain overnight (NWS ~0.1″ of ice) that
lingers on the summit into Friday. Up to 1″ Saturday (models 0–2″).

**Pico.** Same weather as Killington — ice Thursday night into Friday; not yet
open for the season.

**Sugarbush.** Lighter wind Thursday (gusts ~31 mph). Ice Thursday night, and
the summit warms to 34 °F Friday — rain to the top. A dusting Saturday.

**Stratton.** First to see the storm: a wintry mix starts late Thursday morning
(NWS Albany), with gusts above 40 mph Thursday afternoon. Rain to the summit
Friday; around 1″ Saturday.

**Loon.** Windiest on the list Thursday — summit gusts near 48 mph, so expect
lift holds. Rain to the summit Friday (35 °F); about ½–1″ Saturday.

**Cranmore.** Freezing rain Thursday night even on the low summit, rain by
Friday afternoon. Dry and 40 °F Saturday.

**Sunday River.** The calmest Thursday (gusts ~21 mph) and dry until afternoon.
Freezing rain Thursday night that lingers into Friday; dry Saturday.

**Sugarloaf.** Coldest summit (26–28 °F), so it holds on to ice longer than
rain — glaze Thursday night into Friday. About ½″ Saturday.

## Cautions

**Wind.** Gusts above 40 mph Thursday afternoon and evening in western New
England (NWS Albany) — lift holds likely at Loon and possible at Killington,
Pico, and Stratton. **Ice.** Freezing rain Thursday night across the higher
elevations and northern areas; up to 0.1″ of ice. Treat Thanksgiving-night
mountain roads as icy.
